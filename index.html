Smart Convert について

スマート進数変換は、2進数から62進数までをひとつの画面で変換できるアプリです。値を入力すると、ピン留めした進数への変換結果がすぐに並びます。

  • 2〜62進数に対応 — 2・8・10・16進数に加えて、「進数を追加」から好きな進数をピン留めできます。
  • 桁数の上限なし — 任意精度の計算で、64ビットを超える大きな数も丸めずに正確に変換します。
  • タップでコピー — 結果カードをタップしてコピー。長押しで区切り付き・プレフィックス付きのコピーや「この値を入力に使う」が選べます。
  • 履歴 — 変換した値は自動で保存され、タップすると変換画面に戻して再利用できます。

無料でご利用いただけます。アカウント登録は不要です。

About Smart Convert

Smart Convert converts numbers between any bases from 2 to 62, all on one screen. Type a value and the results for your pinned bases appear instantly.

  • Bases 2 to 62 — Binary, octal, decimal and hex by default, and pin any other base with "Add base".
  • No digit limit — Arbitrary-precision math converts huge numbers, well beyond 64 bits, exactly with no rounding.
  • Tap to copy — Tap a result card to copy it, or touch and hold to copy with separators or a prefix, or to use the value as input.
  • History — Values are saved automatically. Tap one to bring it back to the converter.

Free to use, and no account needed.

FAQ:

  • How do I remove ads? — Open the Settings tab and tap "Remove Ads" in the Ads section. After a single purchase, ads are no longer shown.
  • How do I get my purchase back on a new device or after reinstalling? — Sign in with the same Apple Account you purchased with, then tap "Restore Purchases" in the Ads section of the Settings tab.
  • How do I delete history? — In the History tab, swipe left on an item to delete it, or tap "Clear All" to delete everything. The 50 most recent values are kept.
  • Do I have to allow tracking to use the app? — No. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, so the app asks for permission on first launch, but every feature works whether or not you allow it.
